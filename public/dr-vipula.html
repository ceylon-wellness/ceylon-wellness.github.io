Professional profile

A career connecting tourism, diplomacy and wellbeing

Dr. Vipula Wanigasekera’s published biographies describe a professional journey spanning business management, Sri Lankan tourism leadership, diplomatic assignments and higher education. His later public work focuses on meditation, spirituality, wellness coaching and Reiki. The career details below are based on published profiles and should be confirmed with Dr. Vipula before being treated as an official, fully verified biography.

01

Tourism leadership

Published accounts identify senior roles at the Sri Lanka Tourist Board, Chief Executive Officer of the Sri Lanka Convention Bureau (reported 2005–2016), and Director General of the Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority (reported around 2011–2013). Dates and titles require direct confirmation.

02

Diplomacy & management

Biographical sources describe earlier private-sector management experience and diplomatic assignments as Counsellor in Kuala Lumpur and Consul General / head of mission in Oslo. Exact appointment records have not been independently verified for this profile.

03

Academia & writing

Professional references connect him with business and tourism education, including historical associations with Edith Cowan University and Plymouth University. His spiritual writing includes Pointers to Enlightenment and the article Finding Inner Peace. These affiliations are not presented as current appointments.

Training & credentials reported by public profiles: PhD associated with spiritual tourism, master's-level business studies, marketing qualifications, wellness and counselling-related training, and Usui Reiki Master Teacher training. Awarding bodies, exact titles and current professional status should be checked directly. These do not establish medical qualifications.
