# Ceylon Wellness V4 — Wellness-first Trip Planner

## Core direction
- 100% free static planning flow: no paid AI API, no WhatsApp Business API, no login/database required.
- Wellness is the primary journey lens; culture, nature, coast and wildlife enrich the trip.
- Budget-friendly planning is explicit: the visitor chooses comfort/budget level and Ceylon Wellness balances value, wellness and travel time.

## V4 additions
- Arrival + departure date calendars with automatic night calculation and flexible-date option.
- Arrival airport, flight number, scheduled landing time and airport-pickup requirement.
- Traveller type, adult/child counts and optional child ages.
- Wellness goal, accommodation level, interests, transport/guide preference, budget style and travel pace.
- Accommodation options: Budget-friendly, Comfort/3-star, Premium/4-star, Luxury/5-star, Wellness/Ayurveda, or recommend for me.
- Complete WhatsApp handoff brief to the primary contact.
- Advance/refund logic is not invented: final quotation must identify supplier-specific refundable / partially refundable / non-refundable terms and cancellation deadlines.
- Final quotation/invoice happens only after the human-planned itinerary and availability are confirmed and the traveller approves the plan.
